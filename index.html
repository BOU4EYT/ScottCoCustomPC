Payment Terms

  • No parts are purchased without funds received first.
  • Option 1: You buy the parts directly — I assemble and optimize.
  • Option 2: Pay upfront, I source everything and hand you receipts for every purchase.
  • Build labor fee is due at pickup or upon completion.

Used Parts Policy

  • All used parts are stress tested before installation.
  • Used parts require written agreement from client before purchase.
  • Client acknowledges used parts may have a shorter lifespan than new.
  • Used parts typically carry no manufacturer warranty.

1-Year Build Support (Not a Warranty)

This is NOT a parts warranty. This covers my labor only.

  • 25% off labor for troubleshooting and fixes within 1 year of build date.
  • $10 flat fee for quick software issues (drivers, updates, settings).
  • Free advice via text/message within reason.
  • Does NOT cover: Physical damage, liquid damage, power surge, component failure, malware from user activity.
  • Hardware failures are covered by manufacturer warranties only.
